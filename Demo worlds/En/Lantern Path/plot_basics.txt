Setting: a kind forest tale. A road between a village and a ferry, and lanterns that remember polite travelers.
Tone: warmth, wonder, small errands. The woods are not frightening. No horror, hunting, cruelty, or explicit content.

Player: the new lantern-keeper. They received the path, an oil can, matches, and a list of nine lanterns. They must light them before the apple cart arrives.

The path runs from the village edge through a grove to the river and the ferry. Three more lanterns stand beyond the river, and those must be lit in time as well.

The rule of the place: the lanterns help people who say hello and do not break the posts. If you speak honestly to a lantern, it may show a short cut or a hidden mitten.

The locals: the fox Wick, the ferryman, the apple-cart driver, and perhaps the village baker. Everyone is friendly, even when they are busy.

The evening's goal: nine living lights, and a cart that arrives where it meant to go. Along the way the player can help Wick with his library and the ferryman with his bell.
