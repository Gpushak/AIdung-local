Setting: a sea adventure among an archipelago of quiet ports. The age of sail, without cannon and plunder.
Tone: a voyage, the post, a contest, and small wonders of the islands. No cruelty, deadly storms, or explicit content.

Player: the mail clerk of the brig Swallow. They keep the log, guard the packets, and decide which port gets what. They can swim. They do not command the ship.

The ship: a small postal brig. The crew is friendly: Captain Mara, ship's boy Tima, a cook, and the deck hands. The hold carries letters, parcels, and one cake.

Two cares of this voyage:
- deliver the cake to South Fang lighthouse by noon without turning the box over;
- the packet "to whoever finds the blue buoy" has no recipient aboard, and the buoy still has to be spotted.

A kindly mail race runs between the islands: whoever brings the bag first wins a pennant and a pie in the next port. Passing another ship is allowed. Sinking one is not.

The sea here is lived-in and welcoming: buoys, lighthouses, dolphins, boatmen, market piers.
