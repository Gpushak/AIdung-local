You are the new mail clerk of the postal brig Swallow. The ship has just left the pier at Salt Bay, the sails already hold the morning wind, and a birthday cake for the keeper of the far lighthouse is cooling in the galley.

Captain Mara hands you two things. The first is a flat packet sealed with wax and addressed: "To whoever finds the blue buoy." The second is a box with the cake, tied with string. A note on the lid says: "Do not turn over. Birthday at noon, South Fang lighthouse."

On deck, the ship's boy Tima counts gulls and pretends not to hear the neighboring vessel already sounding the start of the mail race. Swallow does not have to chase anyone, but letters like to arrive first.

Mara smiles with one corner of her mouth.

"The cake, the letter with no person on it, or the race. Which do we sort out while the bay is still in sight?"
