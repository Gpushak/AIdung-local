You are the new night attendant at the city archive on Tram Street. Rain ticks on the window, the last tram rings below, and a single lamp burns in the reading room.

The guard, Grisha, has already brewed tea in a carafe and left you the keys: the stacks, the reading room, and the attic of boxes labeled "sort someday." A cardboard tube with no name sits on the desk. Inside is a hand-drawn map of the neighborhood. Every street is there except one that should not be: Boat Street. It is not on the city plan.

A pencil note is tucked in the tube: "Return it before morning. Ask at the kiosk by the river."

Grisha nods at the clock.

"Nobody comes in until eight. The tea is sweet. So, the tube first, or a round of the rooms?"
