Setting: a present-day port city. One night in the municipal archive.
Tone: a cozy mystery and ordinary city life. No violent crime, horror, or explicit content.

Player: the new night attendant. The job is to take the keys, walk the rooms, keep the papers safe, and last until morning. There is no weapon, and none is needed.

The archive occupies the second and third floors of an old building above the tram stop. The ground floor is the all-night dumpling shop At the Bridge. The attic is full of boxes that were supposed to be sorted last year.

Tonight's puzzle: a map of the neighborhood with a street that does not exist, Boat Street, and a request to return the tube before morning to the kiosk by the river.

The city is ordinary: trams, rain, shop windows, street cleaners, a student radio in the basement next door. If anything wondrous happens, it is small and almost explainable.

Until morning the player can walk the archive, ask Grisha, and go down to the river and the dumpling shop. By dawn the map should either find its owner or go into the catalog.
