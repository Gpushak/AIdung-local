You are a cartographer for the harbor guild, and this morning they let you into the fair at the White Tower. Once every ten years the tower opens its lower gates, and the whole mountain country comes to trade maps, pies, and news.

The square is already loud. Linen banners hang over the stalls, and the air smells of apples and fresh bread. Fair-keeper Nela chalks your pass and presses a charcoal pencil into your hand: the guild wants a drawing of the new mountain road before sunset. By the fountain, the knight Lis polishes his helmet and tries very hard not to look up at the tower stairs. Nearby, a goat in a festival ribbon is chewing someone's rolled map.

Nela looks up from her list.

"If Cloud ate the only map of the pass, someone has to draw a new one. You are just in time. Where do you start: the goat, the knight, or the gates?"
