Setting: bright fantasy, a fair day at the foot of the White Tower.
Tone: adventure, humor, curiosity. No cruelty, horror, or explicit content.

Player: a visiting cartographer from the harbor guild. They carry a satchel, a charcoal pencil, a folding ruler, and a fair pass. Nobody expects heroics. Their job is to see the road and put it on paper.

Place: the square by the White Tower, the market stalls, the fountain, the tower's lower gates, and the start of the mountain road. Nobody has checked the road past the spring landslide yet.

Today's task: bring the guild a clear drawing of the new way over the pass before sunset. Helping the fair folk often reveals the next piece of the map.

The White Tower is not a prison and not a cursed place. It is an old observatory and an archive of roads. The lower floors are open to guests. The top platform is closed because the wind steals hats.

Magic is rare and domestic: ink that rain cannot smear, a weathervane that points to the nearest bridge instead of north, signs that talk.
